# ShopNova Sales & Marketing Analysis

## Overview
This project delivers an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution for ShopNova, a multi-category e-commerce company, by analyzing sales, operations, customer, and marketing data for FY 2023. The solution transforms raw transactional data into interactive dashboards that help stakeholders monitor business performance, optimize marketing campaigns, improve customer retention, and enhance operational efficiency.

## Objective
The objective of this project is to solve key business challenges by answering questions such as:

- How is revenue performing across products, categories, brands, and regions?
- Which marketing channels deliver the highest Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) and lowest Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)?
- How can customer retention be improved using Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), Repeat Purchase Rate, and customer segmentation?
- What operational issues, such as return rates and shipping delays, affect business performance?

## Tools Used
- **SQL** – Data cleaning, joins, KPI calculations, and analysis
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard development
- **Tableau** – Data visualization and dashboard creation
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data validation and dashboard prototyping

## Key Insights
- Built a Sales & Operations dashboard to monitor KPIs including Net Revenue, GMV, Average Order Value (AOV), Return Rate, Shipping Performance, Product Performance, and Regional Sales.
- Developed a Customer & Marketing dashboard to evaluate Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), Repeat Purchase Rate, Customer Segmentation, and Marketing Campaign Performance.
- Enabled business users to analyze performance through interactive filters for date, category, brand, region, customer segment, acquisition channel, and campaign channel, supporting data-driven decision-making.

## Dashboard

###  Sales & Operations Dashboard
**Key Features**
- Revenue & GMV Overview
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Sales by Category
- Revenue by Brand
- Top 10 Products by Revenue
- Revenue by State & City
- Order Status Analysis
- Return Rate Analysis
- Shipping & SLA Performance
- Interactive Slicers for Date, Category, Brand, State, Channel, and Payment Method

---

###  Customer & Marketing Dashboard
**Key Features**
- Customer Acquisition Analysis
- Customer Segmentation
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
- Marketing Spend vs Revenue
- Campaign Performance by Channel
- Customer Retention Metrics
- Interactive Slicers for Customer Segment, Acquisition Channel, Campaign Channel, Gender, Age Group, and Region
